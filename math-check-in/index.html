Setting up your own copy

The preview doesn't save answers. The real version runs from a Google Sheet that only you can see, so students never need a new account. Setup takes about 15 minutes, one time.

  1. Make a copy. Click "Get your own copy" above while signed in to your school Google account. Google makes a private copy of the Sheet in your Drive, with the app built in.
  2. Run setup once. In the Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script. Choose setup from the function menu and click Run. Approve the permissions Google asks for. This creates the results tabs.
  3. Deploy the student link. In Apps Script, click Deploy → New deployment, choose Web app, set "Execute as" to Me, and choose who has access (your district's setting may be "Anyone in your organization"). Click Deploy and copy the web app link.
  4. Share the link with students. Post it in your learning management system. Students type their first and last name to start, and can pick up where they left off by typing it the same way.
Running setup and deploying are separate steps. Setup builds the Sheet's tabs. Deploying makes the link students use. If students see an error, check that you deployed as a Web app.
