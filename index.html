Using them well

Three things to know

Your data stays put

The Job Wheel, Behavior Log and Jeopardy save only in the browser you use. Nothing is sent to this website or anyone else. A different computer, or a different browser, starts empty.

Back up now and then

Clearing your browser history can erase saved data. Use each app's backup button and keep the file or text somewhere safe, like your school drive.

Follow your district

Check your district's rules on student data. For behavior records, first names with an initial, or student numbers, work well.

Build your own

No coding. Just describe the routine.

Every app on this page began as a plain-English request to Claude. Here's the recipe.

  1. Name the routine

    Describe the moment in your day the tool is for, and who is in the room.

  2. Say where it runs

    Projector, your tablet or student Chromebooks. It changes the whole design.

  3. List what it remembers

    Names, scores, history. Decide what you're allowed to store.

  4. Try it, then ask for changes

    Use it once with kids, then tell Claude what felt wrong.
